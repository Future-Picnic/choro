# UX Writing · Impeccable edition

Read the interaction path around each string. Identify the fact the person needs now, their next action, and consequences that affect their decision. Use specific action labels and consistent terminology. Errors explain what failed and how to recover without invented causes. Distinguish an empty first-use state from no results, missing permissions, and a failed load. Keep labels visible rather than relying on placeholders. Preserve product facts and domain language, complete translatable messages, accessible names, and room for text expansion. Avoid unnecessary headings and repeated explanations.


Review the affected flow in context. Keep user-requested voice, domain terminology, factual claims, and accessible names intact. Deliver the copy or implement the requested changes without triggering another polish task automatically.
